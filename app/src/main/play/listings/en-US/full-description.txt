RudiMentor teaches drum rudiments on a practice pad. Play along to the click, and the phone's microphone scores every stroke for timing.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 130 lessons on three maps: Singles, Doubles, Paradiddles
• Three tempo ranks for every lesson: Practice, Groove, Stage
• Challenge mode scores your accuracy. Practice mode loops the pattern with no score
• Lesson types: steady patterns, one-hand isolation, unison strokes, sticking and subdivision switches, tempo ramps, weak-hand lead, endurance runs
• Metronome with accents, muted beats and R/L on every beat
• Sound check measures your phone's audio delay, so scoring stays fair

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads, no account, no network. The microphone is analyzed live and never recorded. Progress stays on your device.
