# Privacy Policy — RudiMentor

Last updated: 8 November 2026

RudiMentor is an Android app made by a private individual, Nikolai Krasilnikov
(contact: rudimentor.app@gmail.com). This policy explains what the app does with
data. It is short because the app does very little.

## Data the app collects and shares

**None.** RudiMentor does not collect, transmit or share any personal or device
data. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting
SDK, and it does not request the `INTERNET` permission, so it cannot send
anything anywhere by itself. The only way anything leaves the phone is a
feedback mail that you write and send yourself, described below.

## Microphone

RudiMentor asks for the microphone permission (`RECORD_AUDIO`) for one purpose:
to hear the hits on your practice pad while a level, a practice loop or the sound
check is running, and to score their timing in real time. The metronome does not
use the microphone.

The audio stream is analysed on the device, in memory, as it arrives. It is
**not recorded, not stored in a file, and not transmitted**. When you leave the
practice screen, the stream is closed and nothing about it remains.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores locally, on your device:

- your settings (tempo, sound and calibration preferences, the headphones you
  measured and the names you gave them);
- your progress through the levels (stars, best accuracy, unlocked levels);
- a short technical log of the app's own events (audio output and its name,
  measured latency, errors), kept so that a problem report can explain itself.

The app itself never sends this data anywhere. If Android backup is turned on
for your Google account, Android may include your settings and progress in that
backup, as it does for other apps; the technical log is excluded from backup and
from device transfer. Everything is removed when you uninstall the app or clear
its data in Android settings.

## Feedback and the diagnostic log

"Send feedback" in the About screen opens your own mail app with a draft to
rudimentor.app@gmail.com. The draft already contains the app version, the phone
model, the Android version and the language, so a report is never about an
unknown setup. Nothing is sent unless you send the mail yourself.

If you turn on "Attach a diagnostic log" for that mail, the technical log
described above is attached as a text file, and you can read it first. It
contains no audio, no recordings and no practice results. It can include the
name of your headphones as your phone shows it.

If the app ever closes because of an error, the next start shows that error
with Copy and Share buttons. The report goes only where you choose to share it.

Mails are used only to answer you and to fix the problem. They are not shared
with anyone and are deleted on request: write to the same address.

## Children

The app has no content unsuitable for children, no ads, no purchases and no
communication features. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, of any
age, because it does not collect data at all.

## Third-party services

None. The app bundles open-source libraries and fonts (listed in `NOTICE` and in
the app's About screen); none of them contact a network.

## Changes

If a future version of RudiMentor ever sends data anywhere, this policy will be
updated before that version is published, and the change will be described in
the app's About screen.
